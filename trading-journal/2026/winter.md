# Trading Journal — Winter 2026

## Q2 2026 (April – June)

### Recovery, Pride, and the Cost of Forgetting

The money came back—almost all of it. The account is above **$300K**, which is more than I would earn at Walmart in three months. On paper, that should feel like a win.

It does not. Am I supposed to feel proud? **200% no.**

The reason is simple and uncomfortable: without trading, I cannot cover family expenses and India obligations on a Walmart salary alone. At that income, I could not survive more than two years. Trading is not a luxury for me; it is how I keep the household running. So this recovery is not a trophy—it is a return to baseline, and baseline is still fragile.

### Habits, Attitude, and the Ghost of −$50K

I am slipping again. I can feel it—the same forgetting that has hurt me before. My habits have shifted, and my attitude is not what it was when I was down **$50K**. Back then, fear kept me sharp. Now, with the account green, something loosens. I lose urgency. I lose discipline.

I do not know if I am doing this right or wrong. I do not know if this stretch is karma, consequence, or just the market doing what the market does. I am asking for help—not because I want a miracle, but because I do not trust my own steadiness right now.

### Drinking, Smoking, and Broken Confidence

I am not happy after I drink and smoke. I have tied my mood—and somehow my confidence—to both, and I do not fully understand why. It feels superstitious, or worse, like I am outsourcing control to rituals that do not serve me.

My analysis is often right. I know that. And yet I cannot place trades with conviction. The gap between knowing and doing has never felt wider. I see the setup, I trust the work, and then I hesitate—or I act from the wrong emotional place.

### Note to Self (Account Green)

> Dude—remember: you have only made **2%**. Do not feel unstoppable.
>
> Focus on you. Focus on process. Profits will follow when the person placing the trades is steady, not when the P&L is flattering you into carelessness.
